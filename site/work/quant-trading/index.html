Sentiment-driven
quant trading

A framework that reads market commentary, tests 100+ strategies against it, and routes live orders with risk limits.

Work in progress. Python, NLP, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Groww API. Scroll fast and the candles turn volatile.

Problem

Market commentary comes from many voices: high-net-worth investors, institutions and analysts. It is unstructured text with timestamps, and a trading strategy cannot use it as is.

The text has to become a number, and the number has to earn its place by surviving a proper test before any money moves.

Approach

Three stages, kept separate on purpose. First, score the text. Second, test strategies built on those scores against history, including bad history. Third, execute live with risk controls that can stop the system.

100+
strategies screened
3
strategy families: momentum, mean-reversion, sentiment-arbitrage

Technical
breakdown

  1. Sentiment pipeline

    An NLP pipeline scrapes timestamped HNI, institutional and analyst commentary and converts the unstructured text into entity-level polarity scores. Those scores feed directly into trade-signal generation.

  2. Backtesting harness

    A high-throughput harness screens 100+ quantitative strategies (momentum, mean-reversion, sentiment-arbitrage) against Sharpe, Sortino and drawdown scorecards. It stress-tests them across geopolitical and macro shock regimes, with slippage and latency modelled.

  3. Live execution

    Orders go out through the Groww API using asynchronous, event-driven routing, with volatility-adjusted position sizing and portfolio-level risk circuit breakers.

Stack

Python, NLP, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Groww API.
